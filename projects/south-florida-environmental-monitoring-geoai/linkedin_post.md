🌎 **New Geospatial Project: Environmental Change & Flood Risk Monitoring with GeoAI**

I developed a multi-sensor geospatial workflow to monitor environmental change and potential flood impacts in South Florida using satellite remote sensing, machine learning, and GIS automation.

The project integrates **Sentinel-1 SAR, Sentinel-2, Landsat, and terrain data** to analyze:

- Flood and surface-water change using Sentinel-1 SAR
- Land-cover classification using Sentinel-2 and Random Forest
- Vegetation condition and change using NDVI and NDMI
- Surface-water dynamics using NDWI/MNDWI
- Multi-date satellite time-series trends
- Terrain and environmental variables for GeoAI analysis
- Automated GIS processing and export of spatial products

The broader goal is to create a reusable environmental monitoring framework that can support **disaster response, ecosystem monitoring, climate resilience, land management, and geospatial decision-making**.

**Tools:** Python | Google Earth Engine | ArcGIS Pro/QGIS | Sentinel-1 | Sentinel-2 | Landsat | GeoAI | Machine Learning | GIS Automation
