# Priyanka Belbase | Remote Sensing, GIS & GeoAI Portfolio

Applied, recruiter-facing projects in **agricultural data science, remote sensing, GIS, Python, GeoAI, environmental modeling, validation, and reproducible geospatial workflows**.

> Public demonstration datasets are clearly labeled synthetic where used. They are intended to show methods, software engineering, validation, and decision-support thinking without exposing private or unpublished research data.

## Featured Complete Projects

### 🌱 [Dragon Fruit Yield & Flowering Forecasting](projects/dragon-fruit-yield-flowering-forecasting)
Field experiments + weather + spectral indicators + grouped ML validation for flowering and yield-related forecasting.

### 🛰️ [Geospatial Raster ETL & QA/QC Pipeline](projects/geospatial-raster-etl-qaqc)
Metadata validation, CRS/resolution gates, NoData screening, automated QA reporting, and analysis-ready data handoff.

### 🧠 [Spatial Validation Benchmark](projects/spatial-validation-benchmark)
Random split versus spatial-block cross-validation for defensible geospatial machine-learning evaluation.

### 💧 [Terrain & Hydrology Modeling](projects/terrain-hydrology-modeling)
DEM generation, terrain derivatives, slope, flow-accumulation proxy, stream screening, figures, and tests.
